# 🌍 PLASTIFY  
### Empowering Sustainability, One Plastic Credit at a Time

---

## 📌 Overview
PLASTIFY is a **cloud-powered sustainability platform** that introduces a **Plastic Credit System** to address the growing plastic waste crisis in India. The platform connects **corporates, plastic collection organizations, recycling facilities, and upcycling firms** into a single transparent ecosystem, enabling measurable environmental impact through technology.

By purchasing Plastic Credits, organizations can offset their plastic footprint, fulfill CSR obligations, and actively participate in a **circular economy**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
- India generates millions of tonnes of plastic waste annually with high mismanagement rates.
- Corporates lack a **transparent and standardized system** to offset plastic usage.
- Recycling and upcycling ecosystems remain **fragmented and inefficient**.

---

## 💡 Solution
PLASTIFY offers:
- A **Plastic Credit marketplace** backed by verified waste collection and recycling data.
- A **cloud-based platform** for real-time tracking and CSR reporting.
- Optional **blockchain integration** to ensure transparency and prevent fraud.
- A marketplace for **upcycled sustainable products**, enabling eco-friendly corporate gifting.

---

## 🧱 System Architecture (High-Level)
- **Frontend:** Web-based dashboards for corporates, recyclers, and upcyclers  
- **Backend:** Cloud-hosted APIs for credit issuance, validation, and transactions  
- **Database:** Secure cloud storage for plastic waste and transaction data  
- **Analytics Layer:** CSR reports, sustainability metrics, carbon reduction insights  
- **Blockchain (Optional):** Immutable ledger for Plastic Credit verification  
- **IoT (Future Scope):** Real-time waste tracking from collection centers  

---

## ☁️ Cloud Implementation
- Hosted on scalable cloud infrastructure (AWS / GCP / Azure)
- Serverless automation for Plastic Credit generation
- Secure authentication and role-based access control
- Auto-scaling, backups, and disaster recovery
- Data encryption and compliance-ready design

---
